# Goal
Imagine you are a staff software engineer at a commercial real estate company. The problem I have identified to work on is Loan Surveillance Early-Warning Engine
 
Implementation will be in Java Springboot. 

During my presentation, I need to be able speak about - 

- Why you chose this problem, and who it serves

- What you learned from your research

- How you determined what was worth building

- The key assumptions and tradeoffs you made

- Your architecture and technical decisions

- What you built

- What you'd change or build next
 
A narrow thing that runs beats an ambitious thing that doesn't. Use public or synthetic data as nothing is coming from us, so don't wait on it. Hang onto your prompts and commit history for the harness conversation. And you own what you build; we'll use it only to evaluate your work.
